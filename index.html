THE TAMOKH STANDARD

Why Gogamukh Chooses TAMOKH

We are not a run-of-the-mill garment shop. We built a destination for guys who understand that presence, fit, and self-respect begin with what you put on.

01 ¶

Sculpted Silhouettes & Drape

Every garment on our racks is chosen for its structural proportions. Drop-shoulders that fall cleanly, collars that maintain their frame, and chest cuts that elevate posture.

02 §

Tactile Heavyweight Fabrics

No paper-thin synthetics. We focus on dense, breathable cottons (220–260 GSM), structured knits, and weather-resilient textiles tailored for our local climate in Assam.

03 ◊

Limited Rack Exclusives

Zero bulk uniformity. We stock curated, small-batch runs so you won't bump into ten other people wearing your exact fit around town. When an edition sells out, it’s gone.

04 ✦

THE ORIGIN & PURPOSE

Why We Built TAMOKH in Gogamukh

For years, guys in our town faced a frustrating compromise: settle for cheap, flimsy clothes at standard bazaar stalls, or travel hours away to Guwahati just to find a contemporary silhouette. Even worse: ordering blind from internet apps, only to receive mismatched sizes and scratchy fabrics.

We established TAMOKH at Saikia Market to prove that Gogamukh deserves world-class menswear standards right here at home. We believe true fashion isn't about exorbitant price tags — it's about sharp curation, real fabric integrity, and understanding how clothes should actually fit a man's body.

Every person who walks through our doors is treated like family. No snobbery. No pretension. Just pure dedication to making you feel undeniable every time you step out.
