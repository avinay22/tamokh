OUR STORY & VISION

Why We Started TAMOKH in Gogamukh

For years, people in our town faced the same dilemma: either settle for ordinary clothes from standard bazaar shops, or travel for hours all the way to Guwahati or Dibrugarh just to find modern fashion. Ordering online was another headache — waiting days, only to get an awkward size or thin, scratchy cloth.

We built TAMOKH at Saikia Market to change that for our hometown. We believe that everyone in Gogamukh deserves stylish, high-quality clothes at honest local prices right here at home.

Every person who walks through our doors is welcomed with genuine hospitality. Feel the fabrics in your own hands, try on your favorite fits, and walk out looking and feeling your absolute best.
